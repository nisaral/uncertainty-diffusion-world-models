When does fixing an identifiability failure matter?

Distilling a diffusion world model by matching the teacher’s uncertainty signal is subtle: the matched statistic is one scalar, w* + g* − Σ̄ — two unknowns. This page walks the measured story: the corrected loss’s mechanism transfers across environments (EMA-collapse control 30/30 on DMC), but its practical ordering does not — and that boundary is the paper’s question.

All numbers below are recomputed from the local run files (exact teacher checksums, gap 0.0) by visuals/scripts/build_payload.js. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.

STORYThe verdict in five slides

The DMC 30-seed adjudication (2026-09-08) did not weaken the project — it sharpened it: mechanism transfers, ordering doesn’t, and explaining that boundary is the paper.

V1The identifiability geometry: a strip, not a point

Why “match the teacher’s uncertainty” is not enough. At M=1 (one shared latent) the loss’s sublevel set is a whole line of (w,g) pairs with the same matched scalar; at M≥2 (paired latents) it collapses onto the true point. The freedom is a property of the loss; whether the trade helps or destroys w* is a property of the value map.

V2The collapse, in training curves (DMC sanity, n=10, diagnostic)

Real logged checkpoints at 3k → 15k env steps (last eval record at each milestone; eval-side fields forward-fill between them). Arms sit in a ~0.4–0.6 floor until ~9k and separate late — the concrete reason the old 3.6k budget could not adjudicate.

u_rank_corr over training

Mean teacher–student U rank correlation across the 10 sanity seeds. EMA (identified_hybrid) flatlines near zero/noise; ordinary only jumps at 12–15k.

g ratio (student g / teacher g), log scale — the aleatoric-starvation signal

The EMA-reweighted arm’s aleatoric channel is annihilated almost immediately (ratio ~ 1e-5–1e-4); equal-weight eq tracks the teacher’s g to ~0.6–0.7. Teacher g itself decays over training (SAC reward scaling), so the ratio is the honest signal.

V3The transfer-ordering flip

Same six arms, two environments. On the easy regime (DelayedBimodal, N=10) the combined-fix arm lagged eq tops the table (0.948, 10/10). On the harder regime (DMC hopper-hop, N=30, drifting critic) it drops below eq (0.638, 8/30 — a 7/30 paired reversal) and plain self-gated ordinary wins (0.957, 30/30). The mechanism controls transfer; the practical ordering does not.

Metric legend

metricplain-language meaning
u / teacher Uthe student’s epistemic-uncertainty score per state (MC-UBE local estimate), the object a rollout gate consumes; teacher U is the same object from the frozen teacher ensemble.
wensemble disagreement — variance of value estimates across the 5 teacher members. One of the two unknowns in w* + g* − Σ̄.
galeatoric noise scale (intrinsic stochasticity of the transition). The other unknown. On DelayedBimodal g* ~ 73 vs w* ~ 0.007.
u_rank_corrrank correlation between teacher U and student U over sampled states. 1.0 = perfect ranking preservation. Registered bar: ≥0.70 on ≥70% of seeds.
w_rmseRMSE between teacher and student w on the value map. Different units per environment — rank claims only.
next_state_mseone-step next-state prediction error of the distilled student vs its teacher targets.
final_returnepisodic return of the policy whose imagined rollouts are gated by the student’s U (0.85-percentile stop). Floor-bound on hopper-hop; deferred, not adjudicated.

READ FIRSTCaveats carried into the record

EMA w_rmse skew. On DMC the EMA arm’s w_rmse median is 1.6e-7 but its mean is ~1170 (n=30) — severe skew from a few seeds; w-magnitude claims are rank-only.
Cross-environment comparability. Only u_rank_corr (and the n≥0.70 counts) are compared between DelayedBimodal and DMC here. w_rmse, next-state MSE and return have different units/scales per environment and are shown per-environment only.
Teacher g is non-stationary. Teacher aleatoric scale decays over training (SAC reward scaling), so absolute g at 15k DMC is not comparable to 1,800-step DelayedBimodal runs; the g ratio (student/teacher) is the honest collapse signal.
Returns are deferred, not adjudicated. On DMC all per-arm return means (0.056–0.201) and medians (0.013–0.075) are floor-bound on hopper-hop; the gate-off control is return-neutral (+0.025, CI includes 0). The registered reading: the u-rank story is the whole measurable payoff at 15k.
S1 sanity stays diagnostic. The 10-seed curves on this page are the S1 sanity (diagnostic). Only the 30-seed S2 adjudicates; rows were never mixed across budgets/files.
